# Risk Register — Example

> **Demonstration / Fictional Data — not a real project.**

## Scoring

Probability and Impact are scored from **1–5**.  
Risk Score = Probability × Impact.

| ID | Risk | Cause | P | I | Score | Response | Owner | Trigger / Early Warning |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| R-01 | KPI definitions remain inconsistent | Different reporting practices | 4 | 4 | 16 | Mitigate: approve KPI dictionary | PMO | Conflicting figures |
| R-02 | Key stakeholder unavailable | Operational workload | 3 | 4 | 12 | Mitigate: named backup | Sponsor | Missed reviews |
| R-03 | Scope expands | Additional requests | 4 | 3 | 12 | Control: change assessment | PMO | New deliverables requested |
| R-04 | Adoption remains low | Limited manager involvement | 3 | 5 | 15 | Mitigate: coaching + adoption checks | Operations | Low routine completion |

## Response Logic

**Identify → Assess → Respond → Monitor → Escalate → Review**

## Escalation Rule

Escalate when:
- risk score increases materially;
- trigger condition is reached;
- mitigation is ineffective;
- risk becomes an active issue.

## Review Rhythm

Risk register reviewed weekly during project delivery and at each major decision gate.

> This example uses fictional risks, scores and roles only.
