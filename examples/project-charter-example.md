# Project Charter — Example

> **Demonstration / Fictional Data — not a real project.**

## Project Overview

**Project:** Customer Operations Process Standardization  
**Sponsor:** Operations Director  
**Project Lead:** PMO / Transformation Lead  
**Duration:** 12 weeks

## Business Need

Different teams use inconsistent workflows, ownership rules and reporting routines, creating avoidable delays and limited management visibility.

## Objectives

- Standardize the core operational workflow.
- Clarify roles and handoffs.
- Establish a common KPI structure.
- Introduce a regular governance rhythm.
- Create a sustainable operating baseline.

## Scope

**In scope:** process mapping, role clarification, KPI definition, reporting routine, governance meetings, action tracking.

**Out of scope:** technology replacement, organizational restructuring and customer-contract changes.

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsor | High | High | Steering review |
| Operations managers | High | High | Weekly working session |
| Quality | High | Medium | Process / KPI review |
| Support functions | Medium | Medium | Targeted coordination |

## Success Measures

| Measure | Baseline | Target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Process adherence | 72% | ≥90% |
| Reporting completeness | 65% | ≥95% |
| Open actions older than 14 days | 18 | ≤5 |

## Governance

**Weekly:** project working review  
**Bi-weekly:** sponsor checkpoint  
**Monthly:** benefits / sustainability review

## Key Deliverables

1. Current-state process map
2. Future-state operating model
3. RACI
4. KPI dictionary
5. Governance calendar
6. Action tracker
7. Handover / sustainability pack

## Risks

- Stakeholder availability
- Inconsistent source data
- Resistance to standardization
- Scope expansion

## Decision Gate

Proceed to implementation only after scope, ownership, KPI definitions and governance responsibilities are formally validated.
